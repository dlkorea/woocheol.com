Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

Woocheol Company (the “Company”) complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws, and takes care to protect users’ personal information. This policy applies to the website operated by the Company (woocheol.com) and to the mobile apps provided by the Company (the “Services”). Where an app has its own privacy policy, that policy takes precedence within the app.

1. Personal information we collect and how

The website has no sign-up, login, cookie-based tracking or other feature that automatically collects personal information. We collect the following information only when you contact us by email.

ItemsEmail address, name (if provided), content of your inquiry
MethodEmails you send to us

Information collected by our mobile apps is described separately in each app’s privacy policy and in the Data safety section on Google Play.

2. Purposes of use

  • Reviewing and answering your inquiries
  • Service notices and handling disputes

3. Retention period

Information collected to handle an inquiry is kept for one year after the inquiry is closed and then destroyed. Where the law requires us to keep it longer, we keep it for the period the law sets.

4. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where you have agreed in advance or where the law requires it.

5. Processors

We entrust the following processing work to help us run our business smoothly.

ProcessorWork entrusted
Microsoft Corporation (Outlook.com)Receiving and storing inquiry emails

6. Destruction

Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been met is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

7. Your rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. Email the privacy officer below and we will act without delay.

8. Security measures

We keep access to personal information to a minimum and protect it with account security measures such as two-step verification.

9. Children’s personal information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have collected such information, we delete it immediately.
